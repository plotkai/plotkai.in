Storoxo.com is a next-gen quick commerce site — live, fast, and built to scale. The challenge: deliver a full-stack experience (user signup, email, payments, admin, and more) for under ₹1000/month.

## Byte-to-Byte Optimization

Every line of code was tuned for performance. No bloat, no waste — just pure, efficient delivery. We used non-volatile hosting, so the site stays up and fast, with zero downtime.

## Full-Stack, Fully Integrated

* Razorpay payments built-in
* User signup & email notifications
* Admin dashboard, order management, and more

The result? A quick commerce engine that’s affordable, robust, and ready for scale. Storoxo proves you don’t need a big budget for big impact.
