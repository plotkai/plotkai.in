Most personal finance tools force you into tedious day-to-day receipt scanning, transaction tagging, and backward-looking budget spreadsheets. **FinFunnel by Plotkai** takes a radically different approach: forward-looking **income distribution planning**.

FinFunnel gives you complete clarity on how money flows into your life and exactly where it should be distributed—before it ever gets spent.

## Key Features

### 1. Multi-Source Inflow Aggregation
Consolidate income from all your revenue streams—primary salary, freelance & consulting, stock dividends, rental income, or side hustles. Toggle effortlessly between **monthly** and **annualized** earnings.

### 2. Flexible Outflow & Allocation Planning
Plan money allocation across essential commitments and wealth-building targets. Distribute money either as:
- **Fixed Amounts:** e.g., ₹32,000/mo for rent & maintenance, ₹14,000 for groceries.
- **Dynamic Percentages:** e.g., allocate **25% of total income** directly into SIPs & mutual funds.

### 3. Real-Time Settlement & Savings Rate
Instantly see your **Settlement Balance** (Surplus/Savings) and calculated savings rate (e.g. +37.5%) update in real-time as you tweak earnings or distribution allocations.

### 4. Monthly & Annual Panoramic Overview
Switch seamlessly between monthly cash flow and comprehensive annual projections to understand your full financial picture at a glance.

### 5. Private, Fast & Frictionless
No bank logins, no SMS scrapers, and no invasive transaction tracking. Simply map your money flow, plan your distribution, and execute with confidence.
