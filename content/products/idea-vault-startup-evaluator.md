## Stress-Test Startup Concepts Before Writing a Single Line of Code

Most founders fail not because they couldn't build the product, but because they built something nobody desperately wanted. **IdeaVault by Plotkai** is a structured, analytical evaluation framework designed to help entrepreneurs, product builders, and investors systematically evaluate, score, and prioritize startup ideas.

Grounded in the proven **10-Factor Market Viability Framework** (from Josh Kaufman's *The Personal MBA*), IdeaVault replaces subjective gut feeling with rigorous, evidence-based scoring.

## Key Capabilities

### 1. The 10-Pillar Market Viability Framework
Systematically score each venture idea across 10 critical dimensions (0 to 10):
- **Urgency:** How badly do customers need this right now?
- **Market Size:** How many people actively pay for this type of solution?
- **Pricing Potential:** What is the highest price a customer would gladly pay?
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** How easy and cheap is it to acquire a buyer?
- **Cost of Value Delivery:** What does it cost in money and effort to deliver the value?
- **Uniqueness & Defensibility:** How difficult is it for incumbents to copy?
- **Speed to Market:** How quickly can an initial MVP be built and validated?
- **Upfront Investment:** How much capital is required before the first sale?
- **Upsell Potential:** What secondary offers and expansions are possible?
- **Evergreen Potential:** Will the offer continue generating value with minimal upkeep?

### 2. Guided 4-Step Evaluation Wizard
Move methodically from core thesis definition to ICP profiling, demand estimation, feasibility checks, and pre-launch risk validation.

### 3. Visual Radar Footprint & Heuristic Diagnostics
Generate comprehensive 10-point radar footprints and automated decision badges:
- 🚀 **High Potential (75-100):** Strong market pull and favorable unit economics.
- ⚠️ **Marginal (50-74):** Requires strategic pivots, niche sharpening, or business model tweaks.
- 🚨 **High Risk (&le;50):** High friction, low margin, or poor distribution appetite.

### 4. Executive Evaluation Brief (Print-to-PDF Ready)
Produce clean, publication-grade executive summary reports with diagnostic tables, risk flags, and validation blueprints ready to share with co-founders or investors.

### 5. Privacy-First & Local Storage
All evaluations stay in your browser. Seamlessly back up and restore your entire portfolio using instant JSON export and import.
