## Effortless Baby Tracking for Modern Parents

Caring for a newborn is demanding, exhausting, and filled with questions. Did the baby feed enough? How long was that last nap? When was the last diaper change?

**Babylogs by Plotkai** was created to eliminate the mental clutter. Designed with speed, simplicity, and low cognitive load in mind, Babylogs allows parents and caregivers to log daily baby routines in just one tap—even with one hand in the dark.

## Everything You Need to Monitor Your Baby's Routine

Babylogs unifies daily care tracking with long-term pediatric growth insights into a single, intuitive interface.

### 🍼 Feeding & Nursing
Track breastfeeding sessions with a built-in left/right timer, bottle feeds (formula or pumped milk in ml/oz), and solid food introductions with portion notes.

### 🧷 Diaper & Digestion Logs
Record wet, dirty, or mixed diapers with stool color and consistency indicators to easily spot digestive patterns and keep pediatric visits informative.

### 💤 Sleep & Wake Windows
Monitor naps, night sleep intervals, and awake windows to help establish healthy circadian rhythms and sleep routines for your baby.

### 📈 Growth Trajectory & Milestones
Log weight, height, and head circumference over time with interactive growth curve charts and milestone achievements.

### 💊 Health & Medications
Keep a reliable history of vaccinations, temperature readings during fever episodes, and medicine schedules without missing a dose.

### 👥 Seamless Multi-Caregiver Sync
Keep both parents, grandparents, and babysitters in sync in real time so everyone stays updated on the baby's latest status.

## Why Babylogs?

- **One-Tap Quick Logging**: Minimal taps and large touch targets designed for quick logging during late-night feeds.
- **Privacy-First & Secure**: Your family's sensitive data belongs to you. No intrusive data harvesting, tracking pixels, or third-party ads.
- **Offline Capable & Fast**: Built with modern PWA architecture to work reliably without constant internet connectivity.
- **Dark Mode Friendly**: Soft, non-glaring theme designed specifically for dim nursery environments.
