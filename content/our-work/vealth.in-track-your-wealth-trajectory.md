## Everything you need to manage your wealth

A unified view of all your family's finances in one place.

### Unified Portfolio
Track net worth, equity, mutual funds, gold, property and assets in one simple dashboard with advanced analytics.

### Family Goals
Set and monitor long-term financial goals for your entire family seamlessly. AI-powered insights to reach goals faster.

### Income & Expenses
Keep an eye on your and family's cash flow and ensure you all are saving towards your goals.

## How to use

**Simple & effortless workflow:**

1. **Account & Portfolio Setup (Just Once)**
   Set up your assets, family members, and portfolio accounts.

2. **Income & Expense Entry (Monthly / Weekly / As needed)**
   Track your incoming funds and outgoing expenses over time.

3. **Market Sync & Re-Balancing (Automatic)**
   Market syncs are automatic. Re-Balancing via Harvesting but you have full control.

## Privacy-First

**Built for trust, not attention.**

Obliquity is designed for private portfolio monitoring. Your data stays yours, with end-to-end security and no sharing.

- Private by default
- No ads
- No data selling or linking
- No PII personal data
