FoundersCube - Your Entire Startup Infrastructure - In One Physical Box

FoundersCube — the perfect entry to Information Technology for aspiring and early-stage founders.

We are proud to introduce FoundersCube—a plug-and-play, on-premise Linux appliance that launches your entire corporate infrastructure instantly. Plug it in, map your domain, and go live!

📦 What is FoundersCube?

FoundersCube is a sleek, powerful, dedicated physical hardware appliance that sits right on your desk or or our server racks. Driven by a secure, highly optimized Linux core, it comes completely pre-configured with every critical internal application your growing company needs to operate on Day One.

Instead of managing separate accounts across five different SaaS vendors, FoundersCube unifies your entire business into a single, cohesive ecosystem secured by native Single Sign-On (SSO).

Currently we are offering -

  • Single Sign On (SSO) and user management
  • Company website with SEO and theme engine
  • Knowledge Base to secure company documents and information
  • A carrers page and Application Tracking System (ATS) to hire new talent
  • Project and Task management
  • Team Chat and Collaboration
  • HRM (Coming Soon)
  • CRM (Coming Soon)
  • Invoicing and Expense Management (Coming Soon)
